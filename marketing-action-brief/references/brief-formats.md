# Marketing Brief Formats

Use these formats when turning findings into stakeholder-ready actions.

## Executive Brief

```text
Decision needed:
Recommendation:
Why now:
Evidence:
Risks or caveats:
Next 3 actions:
Owner/date:
```

Use for leadership updates, cross-functional alignment, and quick decisions.

## 30/60/90-Day Plan

```text
First 30 days: fixes, validation, quick wins, baseline measurement.
Days 31-60: experiments, content refreshes, funnel improvements, source cleanup.
Days 61-90: scale winners, build durable programs, report outcomes, decide next bets.
```

Each action should include owner, expected outcome, dependency, and validation metric where possible.

## Presentation Outline

```text
Slide 1: Recommendation and business context.
Slide 2: What changed or what was found.
Slide 3: Evidence and confidence.
Slide 4: Priority actions.
Slide 5: Risks, open questions, and decisions needed.
Slide 6: Timeline, owners, and success metrics.
```

Use one main point per slide. Put details in speaker notes when useful.

## Owner/Action Matrix

```text
Action:
Owner:
Priority:
Due date or sequence:
Expected outcome:
Validation metric:
Dependency:
Confidence:
```

Use when the output needs to become a project plan or next-step tracker.

## Decision Memo

```text
Context:
Options considered:
Recommendation:
Rationale:
Tradeoffs:
Risks:
How we will measure success:
Next decision point:
```

Use when there are multiple plausible paths and the team needs a clear recommendation.

## Prioritization Labels

- **P0**: fix or decide immediately; current data, conversion path, or business decision is materially at risk.
- **P1**: high-impact action with clear evidence or urgent timing.
- **P2**: meaningful optimization or validation check.
- **P3**: polish, monitoring, or backlog item.

## Confidence Labels

- **High**: evidence is specific, source quality is good, and the action is clear.
- **Medium**: evidence is plausible but one validation step is missing.
- **Low**: recommendation depends on incomplete context, small samples, or inferred intent.
- **Blocked**: the next action is to gather missing data before recommending a decision.
