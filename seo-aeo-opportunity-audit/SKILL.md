# SEO/AEO Opportunity Audit

## Overview

Use this skill to turn organic search, content, and SERP evidence into a prioritized opportunity plan. Optimize for B2B SaaS outcomes: qualified demand, buyer education, category authority, answer visibility, and content that is useful to both people and AI systems.

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the business goal, target audience, product/category context, reporting period, comparison period, and source data.
2. Inventory available evidence: queries, pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, position, conversions, content sections, SERP features, competitors, schema, and internal links.
3. Read `references/opportunity-patterns.md` before evaluating opportunities.
4. Classify findings into:
   - **SEO performance**: ranking, CTR, indexation, content fit, internal links, technical blockers.
   - **AEO/GEO readiness**: concise answers, entity clarity, citations, proof, comparisons, FAQs, schema, topical coverage.
   - **Conversion relevance**: whether organic demand reaches a useful next step.
5. Prioritize by business impact, search demand, effort, confidence, and expected path to qualified pipeline.
6. End with a short action plan that names exact pages, queries, sections, owners, and next checks whenever the inputs allow it.

## Output Format

For most audits, return:

1. **Bottom Line**: the highest-value organic growth opportunity and the main risk or uncertainty.
2. **Prioritized Opportunities**: findings with evidence, likely explanation, confidence, impact, and next action.
3. **Query/Page Map**: which queries, intents, and page types should be owned by which URLs.
4. **Content And Answer Recommendations**: answer blocks, FAQ/comparison sections, proof points, schema, and internal links.
5. **30-Day Plan**: specific fixes, refreshes, tests, and validations.
6. **Caveats**: missing data, incomplete periods, tiny samples, source mismatch, or assumptions.

## Prioritization

- Prefer pages and topics that can influence qualified demand, not only traffic.
- Separate branded, non-branded, informational, commercial, comparison, and support intent.
- Treat broad impression growth with falling CTR or weak position as a content-fit problem until proven otherwise.
- For AEO/GEO, prefer concise, citable, well-sourced answers over keyword stuffing.
- Include validation steps before recommending major content rewrites when data quality or intent is unclear.

When only page copy or screenshots are provided, clearly label search-demand and ranking inferences as tentative.
