# CRO Landing Page Review

## Overview

Use this skill to identify conversion friction and turn it into practical page changes or experiments. Optimize for B2B SaaS lead quality, buyer clarity, message match, proof, form completion, and next-step confidence.

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the audience, offer, traffic source, page goal, conversion event, and funnel step.
2. Inventory available evidence: URL, screenshot, copy, analytics, device split, heatmap notes, form fields, CRM quality, and prior tests.
3. Read `references/conversion-friction.md` before evaluating the page.
4. Review the page across:
   - **Message match**: source intent, headline, offer, audience, and promise.
   - **Clarity**: what it is, who it is for, why it matters, and what happens next.
   - **Proof**: credibility, specificity, customer evidence, security, pricing, or outcomes.
   - **Friction**: form, CTA, layout, mobile usability, page speed cues, anxiety, and distraction.
   - **Measurement**: event tracking, funnel visibility, sample size, and lead-quality context.
5. Prioritize recommendations by likely lift, effort, confidence, and risk to lead quality.
6. Produce either direct page recommendations or an experiment backlog, depending on the user's request and evidence quality.

## Output Format

For most reviews, return:

1. **Bottom Line**: the top conversion issue and best next move.
2. **Friction Findings**: prioritized issues with evidence, likely impact, confidence, and recommendation.
3. **Page-Level Recommendations**: specific copy, CTA, proof, layout, form, or mobile changes.
4. **Experiment Backlog**: hypothesis, change, audience, primary metric, guardrail, and expected learning.
5. **Measurement Checks**: tracking and segmentation needed before trusting results.
6. **Caveats**: missing data, small samples, incomplete screenshots, or assumptions.

## Recommendation Rules

- Tie every recommendation to a specific page element, section, form step, traffic segment, or metric when possible.
- Avoid recommending shorter forms by default; consider lead quality, sales routing, enrichment, and qualification needs.
- For B2B SaaS, treat conversion rate and lead quality together.
- Separate fixes that should ship now from tests that need traffic and measurement.
- When evidence is only a screenshot or copy sample, label analytics implications as tentative.

Prefer concrete before/after copy only when the provided page context is sufficient.
