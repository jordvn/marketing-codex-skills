# Search Console Red Flags

Use this reference when reviewing Google Search Console, SEO exports, query/page performance, organic landing pages, or SEO sections of web reports.

## Data Interpretation

- Search Console clicks will not match GA4 organic sessions exactly. Treat directionality and segment patterns as more important than one-to-one reconciliation.
- Current periods can be incomplete because Search Console data lags. Avoid reading very recent partial days as declines.
- Average position is impression-weighted and can move because the query mix changed, not because every ranking changed.
- Query data is sampled and privacy-filtered. Missing long-tail queries do not always mean vanished demand.
- Compare branded and non-branded separately whenever possible.

## SEO Health Red Flags

- Clicks down and impressions down: possible demand decline, ranking loss, indexing issue, or seasonality.
- Clicks down while impressions are flat or up: likely CTR/SERP change, title/meta mismatch, intent mismatch, brand issue, or more SERP features/ads.
- Impressions up and average position down: page may be appearing for broader but weaker queries.
- Average position up but clicks flat: SERP CTR issue, low-volume movement, or query mix distortion.
- A single page with sudden losses: check indexability, canonical, redirect, template changes, internal links, content edits, and status codes.
- Many pages with same-day losses: check robots.txt, noindex, sitemap, canonical patterns, site migration, CMS release, or measurement window.
- Country/device-specific drops can indicate SERP layout, localization, mobile UX, or technical performance issues.

## Content Opportunity Signals

- High impressions, low CTR: improve title tag, meta description, rich result eligibility, intent match, and brand/value clarity.
- Average positions 4-10 with meaningful impressions: refresh content, improve internal links, strengthen entity coverage, add proof, and improve SERP snippet.
- Average positions 11-20 with relevant impressions: expand content depth, create supporting pages, improve topical authority, or consolidate overlap.
- Pages with clicks but weak GA4 conversion: add CTAs, proof points, product pathways, comparison blocks, or offer alignment.
- Pages with conversions but low organic visibility: consider new SEO content, internal links, schema, and paid amplification.
- Queries with answer-like wording: add concise answer blocks, FAQs, definitions, comparison tables, and citation-worthy context for AEO/GEO.

## Cannibalization And Intent

- Multiple URLs ranking for the same query cluster may dilute CTR and authority. Compare pages, intents, and canonical/internal-link signals.
- Blog posts outranking product/solution pages for commercial queries may indicate intent mismatch or weak product page relevance.
- Product pages ranking for informational queries may need supporting educational content rather than heavier sales copy.
- Branded query changes may reflect awareness, PR, paid campaigns, analyst mentions, or product launches rather than SEO execution alone.
